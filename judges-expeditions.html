How the normal game works

In the live game, players use their own GOLD. At least two players fund the target; reaching it starts the voyage. A timed auction follows. The highest bidder gets the treasure, 5% of the sale goes to the house, and 95% goes to contributors in proportion to their funding. A higher bid releases the previous bidder's reserved GOLD. Funding that expires short of the target is refunded.

The current demo configuration allows 5 minutes for funding, an 8-second voyage, and a 5-minute auction. These are configurable testnet durations, not a promise of final production rules. Award ownership does not expire.

Here: funding, sailing, auction and equipping have no time limit. Fund both demo crews, then press Sail, Open auction and Close & split when ready. Settlement requires at least one bid. GOLD and ownership changes are real testnet transactions, confirmed before being shown as final. This is a manual timing variant of the same economic contract, not a test of personal social login or live matchmaking.
